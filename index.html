I want to be clear: I am generally a massive hater. I have spent most of my life cultivating a personality built on finding the "overrated" thing and staying precisely 50 feet away from it. I thought Harry Potter was for people who liked marketing more than magic. I told anyone who would listen—including you—that I’d never watch it.

And then you asked. And suddenly, I’m sitting through *The Sorcerer's Stone*, watching a boy with glasses and a wand, not because the plot finally made sense, but because I would watch a screen of static for three hours if you were the one sitting next to me. My principles didn't stand a chance against you.

That was the moment I realized I was finished. I’m not just a poet; I’m a guy who turned on Instagram notifications for the first time in his life just to see your name. One "niv?" in my chat and the whole world resets. I don't care about being right anymore. I just want to hear my name in your voice.

I tried to be the poet—I told you to "nestle" this one deep inside. I was trying so hard to be smooth, and then I immediately panicked. "Just kidding by the way I’m too bad at this."
