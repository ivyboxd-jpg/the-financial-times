Mission Phases

Phase 1: The Hypothesis (Proposal)

Your proposal must clearly answer these 5 questions to pass the selection gate:

01

Who faces this anomaly?

Target a specific demographic, not "everyone."

02

What is the cost?

Time, money, resources. Make the pain concrete.

03

What is the solution?

Explain your engineered fix in one simple sentence.

04

What is the prototype?

What is the smallest build that proves the science works?

05

Why this crew?

What unique perspective do you bring to this problem?

Phase 2: The Build

  • Prototype, not product: Build one core feature to prove the physics work.
  • Parallel Processing: Pitch Lead works simultaneously. Demo is recorded before the deadline.
  • Energy Management: Take breaks every 90 minutes. Eat. Protect your sleep.

Mission Control & Comms

Stratt didn't build the ship; she gave you resources. Your Mentor works the same way.

  • 30-Minute Rule: Stuck for 30 mins? Ping Discord. Silence is deadly.
  • State the Blockage: Ask "We are deciding between A and B," not "What do we do?"
  • Discord Channels: Use #mission-briefing for updates, #help when stuck, and #launch-pad for crew chatter.

Evaluation

Judges evaluate 5 minutes of your presentation, not 36 hours of code. A brilliant build fails if the pitch doesn't explain why it matters.

1. Problem Definition

Is the anomaly real, specific, and fully understood?

2. Hypothesis

Does the technical approach logically solve the problem?

3. Execution

Does the prototype actually work? (Even one feature).

4. Presentation

Can you brief command clearly in 5 minutes?
