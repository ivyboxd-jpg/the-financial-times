The Story

My career started in Aerospace Engineering — a world where process guarantees result. Math doesn't have moods. Physics doesn't have bad days.

Then I moved into Project Management and hit the wall everyone hits: the biggest variable in any system is the human beings operating it. Brilliant strategies failing not because the logic was wrong, but because the connection was missing.

'Agile' had become a micromanagement weapon. AI had become a source of fear. I pivoted. Today I use my technical background to deconstruct complex org problems, and storytelling to rebuild the culture needed to solve them.
