Designation: Liaison

Volunteer

You are the bridge between the teams and the organizers. You keep the build moving and flag problems before they become disasters.

APR 17 — PREP

Webinar & Briefing

  • Attend the briefing webinar to understand the flow.
  • Learn your team's names and backgrounds.
  • Review the 4 problem statements inside out.
APR 18 — BUILD DAY

Operational Support

  • Ensure the team submits their proposal on time.
  • Check in every few hours—don't wait for them to call you.
  • Escalate any blockers to Raghav or Shradha immediately.

✓ YOUR JOB

  • Be the team's operational anchor.
  • Flag problems early, always.
  • Keep them on time and on scope.

✗ NOT YOUR JOB

  • Make technical decisions for them.
  • Do the actual coding work.
  • Try to solve big issues quietly by yourself.
Designation: Expert

Mentor

You are the subject matter expert. Your job is to help teams think better, sharpen their ideas, and get them unstuck.

BEFORE APR 18

Preparation

  • Read the prep kit and the problem statements.
  • Identify which areas you are most comfortable supporting.
APR 18 — ALL DAY

The Build Phase

  • Be reachable on WhatsApp/Discord for team questions.
  • Proactively check in with your assigned team at least once.
  • Help teams "scope down" so they can actually finish in time.

✓ YOUR JOB

  • Guide teams to think through logic bugs.
  • Ask the questions they haven't thought of.
  • Help them simplify their project to an MVP.

✗ NOT YOUR JOB

  • Write the code for the team.
  • Make final decisions on their behalf.
  • Attend the final presentations.
