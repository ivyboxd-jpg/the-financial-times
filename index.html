LOG // 001

there are billions of celestial bodies out there, but somehow i ended up caught in the prettiest orbit. i’m completely pulled in by your gravity, and honestly? i wouldn’t have it any other way. you're kinda the absolute center of my universe right now.

LOG // 002

i still find it hilarious how we can go from having deep, 2am conversations about life to aggressively debating what to eat for dinner within a ten-minute window. you're an absolute menace when you're hungry, but you're my menace. i wouldn't trade our chaotic little moments for anything.

LOG // 003

and you have this incredibly unfair superpower where you can just look at me a certain way and completely short-circuit my brain. eleven months in, and i'm still out here folding like a lawn chair every single time you smile at me. it’s actually embarrassing. (but also, please don't ever stop).

LOG // 004

did you know the astronaut reid wiseman looked down at earth from the space station, found a massive crater shaped exactly like a heart, and immediately named it after his wife? i finally get it now. because honestly, if i was up here alone, i’d probably try to name the whole damn galaxy after you.

LOG // 005

even when we're just existing together—quietly sitting on a call, going about our days—it feels like we're sharing our own little spaceship. you make the vast, dark, overwhelming unknown feel exactly like home.

so yeah, i'm perfectly happy floating out here, just orbiting you. you're the brightest thing in my sky, and i'm pretty damn lucky to be the astronaut that gets to stare.

happy eleven months, aaradhya. i love you.
