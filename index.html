I’m the kind of person who thinks everything is overrated. If the whole world loves it, I’ve probably spent years explaining why it’s actually quite mid. I hated Harry Potter with a passion I didn’t even earn; I just decided it was too loud, too marketed, too much. I was that guy.

And then you asked me to watch *The Sorcerer's Stone*. And I didn't just watch it—I sat through every single frame with the intensity of someone watching their own house burn down. Not because I liked the wizards, but because you were the one showing it to me. I’d watch a twenty-hour documentary on the history of cement if you asked me to.

That was the moment I realized I was finished. You made my "opinions" irrelevant. One question mark from you—"niv?"—and my brain just reboots. I don't care about being right anymore. I just want to hear my name in your voice.
