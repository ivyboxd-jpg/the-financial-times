The Hypothesis Standard

A good proposal clearly defines WHO has the problem, not just WHAT the problem is.

If you build a brilliant solution for a vaguely defined target audience, you will fail the selection gate. Specificity is survival. Target a real demographic, define their exact pain point, and build the smallest prototype that solves it.
