I’m going to be honest with you: I didn’t have a choice. People like to talk about "falling" in love because it implies a lack of control, a sudden gravity you didn’t ask for. That was me. From the very first day, I was essentially staring at my phone until my eyes burned, waiting for a reply to a story that neither of us probably even remembers now.

But everything really changed when you sent that one message. It wasn't a poem. It wasn't a grand declaration. It was just my name and a question mark. "niv?"

You have no idea what that did to me. Seeing my name in your chat bubble made it feel like a new word entirely. I realized right then that I didn’t just want to see it on a screen; I wanted to hear you say it from across a room, through a doorway, or whispered while I'm half-asleep. I wanted to be the "Niv" that belongs to you for as long as I’m around.
