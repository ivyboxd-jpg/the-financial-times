On being a hater of the overrated, until the girl who notarizes music asks for a favor.

I’m telling you this because I want it on the record: I am not an easy sell. I have spent years cultivating a personality based on hating things that are "overrated." If the world loved it, I probably decided it was too loud, too flashy, or too simple. I thought Harry Potter was the peak of that. I used to tell anyone who’d listen that the Boy Who Lived was just a boy with a marketing team.

And then there was you. You asked me to watch *The Sorcerer's Stone*. And I didn't just watch it; I sat through every minute of it like it was the most vital piece of cinema in human history. Not because I care about Quidditch or wands, but because it was yours. I would watch a blank screen for three hours if you told me it was your favorite color.

That was the moment I stopped pretending. I realized then—and every day since—that I’m finished. Completely. I am the guy who turns on notifications just for one person. I am the guy who reads a single question mark—"niv?"—and feels his heart restart.

the only name that matters now.
